2.0 Introduction
Data is one of the most potent weapons any industry can use to analyse past, present and future
trends in order to compete in the challenging business environment (Gudivada, 2017). Therefore,
data analysis has become popularised as a new strategy in the contemporary business context,
which converts raw data to meaningful visualisation and provides meaningful interpretations to
identify ongoing and future business issues/challenges. Moreover, logical data analytical
prediction creates more protection for the organisation, allowing it to be ready for future potential
challenges and overcome them with appropriate predefined solutions (Gudivada, 2017). Therefore,
this novel strategy is now being used by many organisations rather than traditional decisionmaking methods to compete in complex business issues and find new avenues for solving business
problems efficiently and effectively (LaVange, 2014).
Accordingly, the financial sector is one of the leading industries that use data analytics for
decision-making in the contemporary era. Fintech is a novel financial, technological concept that
challenges the demanding banking industry by providing simplicity and convenience to customers,
influencing the use of other financial applications to cater to banking needs (Adamek & Solarz,
2023). Moreover, Fintech facilitates many financial services and provides opportunities across the
globe, especially for customers who are in remote areas (Utami & Supriadi, 2023).
Lending Club is an organisation incorporated in 2006 in California, USA, with the intention of
helping people with their financial needs; it provides loans to individual borrowers while opening
an avenue for investors to invest in the borrowings (Lending Club, 2024). Therefore, this
mechanism is called peer-to-peer lending (P2P), and LC acts as a connecting platform for both the
borrower and investors (Lending Club, 2024). Moreover, LC evaluates the credibility of the
applicants and passes the findings to investors for decision-making on their investment. LC only
makes interest and fee income from borrowers and pays back investment returns to the investors
on a monthly basis (Lending Club, 2024). Besides, this Fintech Lending and investing application
was acquired by Radius Bank and contributed to the national economy of the USA by enhancing
its lending portfolio to five billion US dollars by the end of the first half of 2014 (Lending Club,
2024).
Since this Lending Club provide lending from the investor’s capital, it is the responsibility of the
organisation to pay back interest and capital on a timely basis to protect the investor's interest and
keep optimum liquidity in the business. Therefore, it is important to identify trustworthy 
prospective borrowers to ensure the timely collection of loan proceeds to safeguard investor
interest (Ahmed et al., 2021). Therefore, data analytics is prominent in identifying prospective
borrowers and finding new lending opportunities for Lending Club to maximise profits and
investor revenue.
Therefore, this report discusses two burning issues of Lending Club: Identifying key factors that
influence the credit risk associated with fintech lending for LC and Identifying factors that drive
investor interest in a quality lending portfolio within the framework of the data analysis. Moreover,
this study used descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive data analysis methods to find
solutions for business problems.
Section three of the report provides an insightful description of the data set and its attributes. In
contrast, section four critically discusses the framed business problems and illustrates how data
analytics provide data-driven solutions for contemporary business issues. Finally, the conclusion
summarises the report's findings to complete the report.
3.0 Data Description
The data set is a collection of data that consists of columns and rows, where columns represent the
variables of the data set while rows represent the related data to one particular data variable
(LaVange, 2014). This data is mainly categorised as numerical and categorical data, which are
further divided into four main categories: nominal, ordinal, interval, and ratio. Identification of the
variables and a clear understanding of the data set variables influence the use of correct data
analysis methods to accurately reflect the data problems of a contemporary business context
(LeMire et al., 2016).
This database is all about the lending database of Lending Club, which is found on the Kaggle
website (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/wordsforthewise/lending-club), holds issued, settled,
current, late and non-performing loans for the period from 2007 to 2018. Over 1.048 Mn rows and
151 data variables were found in the database, and all the data types were found there.

cleaned with the anticipation of finding meaningful interpretations for the data problems (Ng &
Winkler, 2014). Consequently, data was filtered to get the latest calendar year data for 2017.

rows were reduced to 500K, and further cleaning was performed thereafter by carefully analysing 
all the variables and reducing to fifty-eight variables (columns). Some empty rows were filled with
zero, and some data rows were removed entirely, which did not have data for important data
variables. Focusing accurate analysis results for regression and Rapid minor, data was cleaned
aiming the missing values in the crucial numerical data fields. This exercise was performed using
pivot tables, Excel functions, and formulas to get the final data set with 212,270 data rows.
Considering the requirement of analysis, new columns were added to the dataset, such as joint
annual income, joint DTI ratio, average FICO score (credit rating) and monthly loan instalment.

4.0 Data-driven decision-making for lending institutions
With the acceleration of digitalisation, new fintech solutions influence banking operations. Fintech
lending is more popularised with automated data-driven decision-making (DDDM) using even
machine learning algorithms (Gul & Al-Faryan, 2023). In context, DDDM has been popularised
as a new strategy among organisations, which promotes more precisely identifying opportunities
and business issues (Marikar & Bandara, 2020). In view of these benefits, lending organisations
started aggressively using this new strategy in the competitive business environment, considering
its benefits. In contrast, lending is a profitable business to lending organisations, but there is a high
tendency to lose the revenus due to bad loans in the lending portfolio. Therefore, data-related
findings support identifying lending risk, profitable products, quality customer segments, new
product innovations, and market demands to compete with rivals in challenging environments
(Gul & Al-Faryan, 2023). Therefore, two critical data problems related to LC are identified and
analysed based on data analytics techniques to find the root course of the problem and provide
solutions using DDDM.
4.1 Identify key factors that influence the credit risk associated with fintech
lending for LC
Even though Fintech lending is a novel concept in the financial industry, lending is a traditional
fund-based financial product for lending organisations. Since this LC platform influences with
Peer to Peer (P2P) lending mechanism, supply and demand are created through the Internet, and
investors fund loans without collateral even without knowing the borrowers (Serrano-Cinca et al.,
2015). Therefore, it has a high tendency to default on loans by the borrowers. Thus, loans not paid
for a considerable time frame are considered bad loans, and LC sets off those loans, causing
investors a loss of capital investment and interest income. Since fintech solutions are widely spread
over new financial solutions, it is important to promptly secure the investor's interest, addressing
Fintect-related issues (Reddy & Gopalaraman, 2016). Moreover, the loss of capital causes
financial losses to investors and a loss of confidence decrease the interest in Fintech applications.
In contrast, this loss of stakeholders' interest may even lead to an impact on the profitability of the
LC.
Using data analytics to illustrate this lending problem provides statistical interpretations that
provide a measurable understating of the problem statement and allows logical decision-making
(Marikar & Bandara, 2020). Moreover, by using data analytics methodologies, it is essential to
identify the variable's impact on influencing the credit risk of the borrowers to identify prospective
borrowers through the Fintech lending platform. Accordingly, Tabel 1 illustrates the list of
variables used to identify the data problem.

4.2 Identifying factors that drive investor Interest in a quality lending portfolio
LC influence P2P lending through Fintech applications where investors are the key stakeholders
funding the organisation to make a profitable income. Therefore, investor interest is significant,
and LC is primarily responsible for safeguarding investors by providing profitable investor
opportunities (Lending Club, 2024).
P2P lending is always influenced by the investor’s decision-making. Setiawan et al. (2020) argued
that investor decision-making is not always logical as decision-making is more considered
socialistic factors like the borrower’s first impression. Therefore, it is essential to investigate the
factors that influence decision-making on P2P lending to protect investor interest and influence
investors to make effective decision making on lending investments. Luo et al. (2011) argued that
addressing investor problems is more curtail than losing investor interest, which can also
negatively impact the business operation of LC.

of the occurrences while predictive analysis can predict the future trend of the occurrence of the
negative implications and further illustrate future prospective investor opportunities. Therefore,

RepidMionr clustering is more suitable for predictive analysis, which uses machine learning

RapidMinor clustering analysis also interprets the investor behaviour on investor decisions. As

In reference to Setiawan et al. (2020), investor decision-making is mainly based on the return on
investment and the risk involved in the investment opportunity. Moreover, investment decisionmaking is further influenced mainly by three factors: information provided by the lending
platform, the first impression of the borrower and social and emotional influences (Setiawan et
al., 2020). Besides, more investors are not literate about financial evaluations, and their investment
decisions are mainly based on basic evaluation methodologies such as income, savings balance, 
employment experience and satisfactory bank card details. However, significantly less
consideration is given to the borrower’s DTI ratio and FICO credit score (Lending Club, 2024a).
Therefore, LC is primarily responsible for filtering prospective borrowers and providing profitable
investment opportunities to investors (Luo et al., 2011). Thus, it is recommended that LC share
the primary knowledge with investors to identify prospective borrowers and make profitable
investments.
5.0 Conclusion
Data analytics is a novel strategy that organisations use to be competitive in demanding markets,
providing clear and accurate findings to identify and interpret real problems in a business
environment. Moreover, it opens new avenues for finding solutions to business issues.
Consequently, two data problems of LC are discussed in this report, namely identifying the factors
that influence the credit risk in LC and identifying factors that drive investor interest. Therefore,
many of the analysis techniques, such as descriptive, diagnostic, and predictive, were used to
understand the data problems of LC, and it was found that the findings are valid and need to be
actionable to improve revenue and safeguard investor interest.
Moreover, LC lending evaluation must be improved to maintain a healthy loan portfolio. Thus,
the DTI ratio and income verification are proven to be evaluated for future lending eveluations,
whereas LC can do more marketing activities to promote Green and vacation loans, which enhance
the healthy loan portfolio and reduce the credit risk to the organisation. Considering the investors
as the primary stakeholders, it is important to provide them with a prospective lending database in
which to invest, expecting reasonable profits upon investment.
Accordingly, problems have been addressed by using data analytics, and LC has the opportunity
to strengthen its strategies to penetrate new markets and enhance revenue with the new product
development, which will be profitable considering risk factors involved with the current lending
opportunities.
