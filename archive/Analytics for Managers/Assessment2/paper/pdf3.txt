1. Introduction
Bangalore is Karnataka's capital and largest city, a state in southern India. It is India's third
most populated city, with a metro population of almost 11 million. Bangalore is renowned for
its extensive restaurant scene and diversified food culture, providing a gourmet haven for
food lovers. In today's competitive restaurant industry, making informed decisions is crucial
for the success of a new establishment. With the rapidly expanding restaurant scene in
Bangalore, India, choosing the right location, managing prices effectively, and maintaining
high levels of customer satisfaction are critical challenges aspiring restaurant owners face.
Fortunately, business analytics offers valuable tools and techniques to address these
challenges and make data-driven decisions. By leveraging data from sources like Zomato, a
popular online food delivery and restaurant discovery platform, restaurant owners can gain
insights into the market landscape, customer preferences, and industry trends.
This article explores the application of business analytics in opening a new restaurant in
Bangalore. It begins by discussing the dataset obtained from Zomato, which provides
comprehensive information about restaurants in the city. The data cleaning process is then
outlined, ensuring accuracy and reliability for subsequent analysis. Three critical problems
faced by restaurant management are identified: choosing the location and restaurant type,
price management, and maintaining customer satisfaction levels.
Using business analytics techniques, each of these problems is examined in detail, providing
actionable insights for decision-making. For selecting the optimal location and restaurant
type, chi-square test and visualizations like bar charts are employed to identify areas with
high restaurant density and understand the association between location and restaurant type.
Price management is addressed through market research and analysis of pricing strategies,
utilizing chi-square test and line charts to identify the relationship between location and price
range. Lastly, how to maintain customer satisfaction levels is explored by analyzing online
ratings and reviews, leveraging descriptive statistics and bar charts to gauge customer
perception and identify areas for improvement.

4.1 Choosing the Location & Restaurant Type
One of the most crucial factors in a restaurant's success is finding the perfect location, and
choosing the wrong location could result in a lack of sales and ultimate failure (Calderón et
al., 2021). It is also one of the characteristics that's difficult to change and nearly impossible
to overcome (Calderón et al., 2021). By drawing in more customers, a good location can
boost a restaurant's competitive advantage and influence the restaurant that customers select
(Torlak et al., 2020). According to Self et al., (2015), restaurant failure has long been
attributed primarily to bad location decisions. The main problem with picking the wrong
location for a restaurant is that after it has opened and sales are lower than expected, the
investment in infrastructure or adaptations has already been made and cannot be altered (De
Meirleir, 2011, as cited in Calderón et al., 2021). However, even if a restaurant's strategy was
poorly planned, a good location might still contribute to its success (Karadeniz, 2009, as
referenced in Calderón et al., 2021).
When opening a new restaurant, it is crucial to consider the existing restaurant landscape in
the chosen location. Opening a restaurant in an area with an already established restaurant
scene can be advantageous, as it indicates a demand for dining options and attracts potential
customers (Self et al., 2015). However, it is crucial to choose the type of restaurant carefully
based on the location (Prayag et al., 2012). Understanding the preferences and demographics
of the target customer base in the area can help in selecting a restaurant type that aligns with
their needs and desires (Calderón et al., 2021; Prayag et al., 2012). This strategic alignment
between the restaurant type and location can increase the chances of success and customer
attraction, even in the presence of competition.

4.2 Price Management
Price management is a crucial and challenging decision for new restaurants. Determining the
right pricing strategy requires careful consideration of various factors to ensure the
restaurant's success. Setting prices too high may deter potential customers, while setting them
too low may lead to financial instability or undervaluing the offerings (Liu & Tse, 2018). One
of the critical considerations in price management is understanding the target market and its
willingness to pay. Conducting market research and analyzing the pricing strategy of already
established restaurants can provide insights into the customer’s price sensitivity and
expectations (Raab et al., 2009). This information allows restaurant owners to select an
approximate price range that aligns with the target market's affordability and perceived value
(Dominique-Ferreira et al., 2019; Raab et al., 2009).
Moreover, it is important to consider the restaurant's positioning in the market. Factors such
as the restaurant's concept, type, and location must be reflected in the pricing strategy
(Thielemann et al., 2018). For example, a higher-end fine dining restaurant may justify higher
prices by emphasizing premium ingredients and an exceptional dining experience. On the
other hand, a casual eatery might opt for a more affordable pricing structure to attract a larger
customer base (Liu & Tse, 2018). Carefully selecting an appropriate price range not only
attracts the target customers but also contributes to the restaurant's profitability. Overall,
Price management poses a significant challenge for new restaurants. By understanding the
target market, considering the restaurant's positioning, and carefully selecting an approximate
price range, management can establish a pricing strategy that attracts customers, reflects the
restaurant's value proposition, and supports long-term success (Dominique-Ferreira et al.,
2019; Liu & Tse, 2018; Thielemann et al., 2018).
A chi-square test can be utilized to identify the relationship between restaurant location and
the approximate price range of restaurants. This statistical test examines the association
between two categorical variables and can help determine if there is a significant relationship
or dependence between these variables of interest. In this case, the chi-square test can assess
whether there is a relationship between the location of restaurants and the price range they
offer.

4.3 Maintaining customer satisfaction levels
Maintaining customer satisfaction levels is paramount for new restaurants as it directly
influences their success and long-term viability. Satisfied customers are more likely to
become repeat customers, recommend the restaurant to others, and leave positive reviews,
which can significantly contribute to the establishment's reputation and overall growth
(Vajjhala & Ghosh, 2022). However, ensuring customer satisfaction can be highly
challenging for new restaurants. They face various hurdles, such as establishing a solid
reputation, meeting customer expectations, and managing operational complexities (Bilgihan
et al., 2018). Building a positive reputation takes time and effort, as new restaurants need to
prove themselves to a skeptical audience. They must strive to exceed customer expectations
from the moment they open their doors, as first impressions are vital in shaping customers'
perceptions (Lee & Kim, 2020). Any shortcomings or inconsistencies in the restaurant can
result in dissatisfied customers and negative reviews, which can be detrimental to the
restaurant's success (Vajjhala & Ghosh, 2022).
Analyzing online ratings of existing restaurants can provide new restaurants with valuable
insights to solve various problems and improve their chances of success in the competitive
industry. Data analysis of online ratings of existing restaurants allows new restaurants to gain
a comprehensive understanding of customer preferences and expectations (Bilgihan et al.,
2018; Vajjhala & Ghosh, 2022). By examining positive reviews, new establishments can
identify the aspects of their competitors' offerings that are highly praised by customers (Lee
& Kim, 2020). This insight enables them to replicate successful elements, such as popular
dishes, outstanding service, or unique ambiance, in their own establishment. Understanding
what customers value most in their dining experience helps new restaurants tailor their
offerings to meet those expectations and create a competitive advantage (Bilgihan et al.,
2018; Vajjhala & Ghosh, 2022). On the other hand, studying negative reviews in online
ratings provides new restaurants with crucial information about the shortcomings and
weaknesses of their competitors. By analyzing common complaints or issues raised by
customers, new establishments can proactively address those areas and ensure they do not
make the same mistakes.

5. Conclusion
In conclusion, leveraging business analytics can significantly benefit aspiring restaurant
owners in making informed decisions when opening a new restaurant in Bangalore. By
analyzing comprehensive data from sources like Zomato, restaurant owners can gain valuable
insights into the market landscape, customer preferences, and industry trends. This article
highlighted three key challenges restaurant management faces: choosing the location and
restaurant type, price management, and maintaining customer satisfaction levels.
Each of these challenges has been addressed through the application of business analytics
techniques. For selecting the optimal location and restaurant type, tools such as bar charts,
chi-square test, and Mekko charts have been utilized to identify areas with high restaurant
density, understand the association between location and restaurant type, and identify the top
restaurant types in specific locations. These insights enable restaurant owners to position their
sample
new restaurants strategically, align with customer preferences, and maximize their chances of
success.
Price management, another crucial aspect, has been tackled through market research, analysis
of pricing strategies, chi-square test, and line charts. By understanding the target market,
considering the restaurant's positioning, and analyzing pricing trends in different areas,
restaurant owners can establish an appropriate and profitable price range that attracts
customers and reflects the restaurant's value proposition.
Finally, maintaining customer satisfaction levels has been addressed by analyzing online
ratings and reviews. By examining positive reviews, new establishments can identify
successful elements in their competitors' offerings and replicate them in their own
establishment. Analyzing negative reviews helps identify areas for improvement and allows
proactive measures to address customer concerns and ensure a positive dining experience.
By leveraging business analytics, aspiring restaurant owners can make data-driven decisions
that mitigate risks, position their new restaurant strategically, set competitive prices, and meet
customer expectations. These insights contribute to the restaurant's overall success, growth,
and profitability in the dynamic and competitive restaurant landscape of Bangalore. In an
industry where informed decision-making is crucial, business analytics offers valuable tools
and techniques to gain a competitive edge and ensure long-term success in the vibrant
restaurant scene of Bangalore.
